# CarLab — credits and source register

Maintained by 351labs. Bibliography consolidated on 1 November 2026 from the laboratory's research records of 30–31 October 2026 and checked against the current Rust/TypeScript source. This register includes incorporated material, methodological references and investigated alternatives. Inclusion does **not** mean that a paper's implementation, coefficients, dataset or claimed accuracy was adopted.

CarLab's original code and documentation are licensed under **Apache-2.0**, as supplied in `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`. This grant does not relicense third-party software, scientific coefficient data, measured data, recordings, publications or trademarks. Full upstream notices are preserved separately. The canonical register is [351labs/carlab/credits.md](https://github.com/351labs/carlab/blob/main/credits.md); copies are included in the web, native and TypeScript/WASM distributions.

## 1. Contribution and implementation boundaries

351labs and contributors created the laboratory's Rust/TypeScript implementations, procedural DSP, parametric 3D components, interface and translations. The synth generates its engine waveform procedurally; the reference recordings are not sampled into the generator. The parametric components are original explanatory geometry, not manufacturer CAD, verified engineering drawings or commercial game assets.

| Component | Main source files | Relationship to external sources |
|---|---|---|
| Four-stroke, crank-angle cylinder cycle, finite Wiebe heat release, valve flow | `native/src/cylinder_cycle.rs`, `cylinder-cycle.ts` | Original reduced implementation; references below inform physical structure. Fitted values are not a universal engine calibration. |
| NASA-7 gas properties, fixed products and rich water-gas shift | `native/src/gas_properties.rs` | **Seven coefficient sets incorporated from Cantera 3.1.0**, with the provenance, changes and BSD notice below. Cantera's solver is not a dependency. |

Historical research notes preceded several implemented options. In particular, older statements that NASA data or Woschni were only proposals no longer describe the optional Rust model. The TypeScript legacy approximation and the Rust NASA-7 mode are different models.

## 2. Incorporated thermodynamic coefficient data

**Bonnie J. McBride, Sanford Gordon and Martin A. Reno (1993). _Coefficients for Calculating Thermodynamic and Transport Properties of Individual Species_. NASA TM-4513.** [NASA record and report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19940013151). The record identifies it as a US government work with public use permitted. This is TM-4513, not the different NASA RP-1311 report mentioned as background in early research.

The immediate coefficient source is **Cantera v3.1.0, `data/nasa_gas.yaml`**, [tagged source](https://github.com/Cantera/cantera/blob/v3.1.0/data/nasa_gas.yaml). Its header explicitly credits TM-4513 and identifies the [Caltech thermodynamic database distribution](https://shepherd.caltech.edu/EDL/PublicResources/sdt/thermo.html). Retrieved file: 276,140 bytes; SHA-256 `4de6199d65d2d3db782e30573720c723130953707336add59713b02d8667e4db`. The seven source species are N2, O2, Ar, CO2, H2O, CO and H2, covering 200–6000 K. Six have fits split at 1000 K; Ar has one constant fit, duplicated across the code's two ranges. The tag describes the source distribution; the YAML itself was generated with Cantera 2.6.0, as its header states.

CarLab selects those species, computes mole/mass mixture properties, shifts the high-range enthalpy integration constant to enforce continuity at 1000 K, and uses a sensible-energy reference of 298.15 K. The rich-product water-gas-shift freeze temperature of 1700 K is a declared CarLab assumption, not a fitted NASA engine result. No chemical kinetics, dissociation, NOx model or general chemical-equilibrium solver is provided by this subset. Dry-air fractions are identified in code with the [US Standard Atmosphere 1976 convention, NOAA-S/T-76-1562 / NASA-TM-X-74335](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19770009539); the universal gas constant is identified with [CODATA 2018, NIST publication](https://srd.nist.gov/jpcrdreprint/5.0064853.pdf).

The full [Cantera BSD-3-Clause notice](licenses/cantera-BSD-3-Clause.txt) is preserved verbatim from [v3.1.0 `License.txt`](https://github.com/Cantera/cantera/blob/v3.1.0/License.txt), SHA-256 `469fa95f76e0c652b4f204c2e5d3ffd184dbbb85b2a7fbc75355bee3a16e48e2`. Its copyright holders are California Institute of Technology, Sandia Corporation and Cantera Developers. This notice is also included in native/API and web distributions. [Cantera's species thermodynamic documentation](https://cantera.org/stable/reference/thermo/species-thermo.html) explains the polynomial representations.

## 3. Incorporated measured data and geometry context

**US EPA / National Vehicle and Fuel Emissions Laboratory, _2014 Mazda 2.0L SKYACTIV engine, Tier 2 fuel test data package_, archive dated 29 March 2018.** [Original archive](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2018-04/2014-mazda-2.0l-skyactiv-engine-tier-2-fuel-test-data-package-03-29-18.zip). The package report identifies Version 2018-02, revised 28 March 2018. Archive: 5,866,485 bytes; SHA-256 `01895c570c01303dccbaa85fced4dcea60177bef0119564f1874e821c202c8a2`.

`validation/mazda-measured-all.json` contains 137 extracted steady-state numeric rows; `validation/mazda-measured.json` preserves a 12-point subset. Workbook/report member identities, hashes, field meanings and extraction provenance are retained in the fixtures and `validation/import-provenance.json`. CarLab adds field mapping, derived quantities, splits and model comparisons; it does not distribute the original ZIP, report figures, manufacturer photographs or ECU firmware. The particular fuel, test boundary, warm operating conditions and limited RPM coverage matter. Rated peak specifications are not measured rows.

The package's Discussion and Data Usage section permits broad use of engine-operation/fuel-consumption data, as recorded in the extraction research. The [EPA benchmarking catalogue](https://www.epa.gov/vehicle-and-fuel-emissions-testing/benchmarking-advanced-low-emission-light-duty-vehicle-technology) supplies the measurement context. No dedicated blanket license file was identified in the archive: the [EPA disclaimer catalogue](https://www.epa.gov/web-policies-and-procedures/epa-disclaimers) makes document-specific and third-party qualifications relevant. The limited numeric extraction is attributed to EPA; **Apache-2.0 is not asserted as its source-data license**. Full-attachment redistribution requires its own notice review.

**Ellies, Schenk and Dekraker, SAE 2016-01-1007 (2016), _Benchmarking and Hardware-in-the-Loop Operation of a 2014 MAZDA SkyActiv 2.0L 13:1 Compression Ratio Engine_.** [EPA-hosted manuscript](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2016-10/documents/2016-01-1007-benchmark-hil-operat-2014-mazda-skyactiv-2.0l.pdf). Context for the measured engine and test setup.

**Mazda North American Operations, _2014 Mazda3 specification deck_, filename revision v2014.6.** [OEM PDF](https://news.mazdausa.com/download/MAZDA3-14MY-SpecDeck-082613-v2014.6-for-press.pdf), engine table, PDF page 13. Geometry cross-check for the 2.0 L variant; not a teardown measurement or a complete set of cylinder/valve/material inputs.

**Dekraker, Barba, Moskalik and Butters (2018), _Constructing Engine Maps for Full Vehicle Simulation Modeling_, SAE 2018-01-1412.** [EPA complete-map explanation](https://www.epa.gov/vehicle-and-fuel-emissions-testing/combining-data-complete-engine-alpha-maps). Complete ALPHA maps can combine measurements and estimates. They must not be relabeled as measurements at every cell.

## 11. Attribution maintenance and unresolved provenance

Keep sources attached to imported data/maps, recording transformations and calibration versions. New recorded samples need their own author, original URL, exact license, transformations and checksum. New coefficient datasets need the exact version and upstream notices. Publicly readable papers and commercial demos are not automatically reusable datasets or code.

The following gaps are recorded rather than filled with invented claims: several research leads have abstract/index access only; some author/year/DOI metadata remains unresolved; source-specific reproduction rights were not established for those leads; the EPA archive has document-specific data-use language rather than a blanket SPDX license. No restricted research tables, figures, models, learned weights or commercial recordings are redistributed from those leads. Adversarial review must assess the concrete release artifacts and incorporated-data scope before a public-readiness claim.
